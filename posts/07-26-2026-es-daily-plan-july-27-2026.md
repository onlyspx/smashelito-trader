The auction has established a 2-day balance, which is now of short-term interest. The key question is whether sellers can continue to accept lower prices following Thursday’s gap lower and maintain downside pressure.

Intraday strength would be indicated by a reclaim of 7485 (UT1), while weakness would be signaled by a break and hold below 7426 (DT1), Thursday’s VAL, where notable aggressive selling activity was absorbed.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is at **7458**, Friday’s Initial Balance high, coinciding with the FS VWAP. Holding below 7458 maintains downside pressure, targeting Thursday’s VAL at **7426 (DT1)**. Acceptance below 7426 would signal intraday weakness, targeting **7403 (DT2)**, with **7362 (FDT)** serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.

**On the flip side**, reclaiming and holding above **7458** shifts focus to **7485 (UT1)**, with the unfilled gap at **7525 (FUT)** serving as the final upside target under sustained buying pressure.
