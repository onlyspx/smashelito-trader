**On the flip side**, failure to hold 7472 would shift focus to **7442 (DT1)**. Acceptance below 7442 would signal intraday weakness, targeting the Weekly Extreme Low at **7405 (DT2)**, with **7380 (FDT)** serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.
