### Contextual Analysis & Plan

During the overnight session, buyers attempted to gain meaningful traction above 7747 (Smashlevel), which they ultimately struggled to do. The auction revisited both 7718 (DT1) and 7772 (UT1) overnight, with both levels finding an opposite response, meaning a reaction against the direction of the initial move into the level (see **Figure 1**).

Why? Because doing so would reject Tuesday’s value and break the pattern of developing lower value that has been in play this week. Buyers defended 7747 (7748) during the A-period, resulting in a move toward 7772 (UT1). So far, the market has remained contained between 7718 and 7772 this week, two levels also highlighted in the [Weekly Plan](https://newsletter.smashelito.com/p/es-weekly-plan-sep-28oct-2-2026).

After tagging 7772, the upside momentum notably stalled, and the auction saw two-sided trade for most of the session. When the auction returns to an area of prior value, such as the HVN at 7772, it’s not uncommon to see this type of behavior. Buyers and sellers have previously found agreement around this price, so revisiting the area can naturally lead to renewed two-sided trade rather than immediate continuation.

The tone changed during the last 30 minutes of RTH, referred to as the M-period, as we saw a closing liquidation break. The key question now is whether value will follow price or if the auction will rotate back toward established value. Tomorrow’s value development will therefore be important to monitor, particularly in relation to today’s highlighted spike area.

After building value above Tuesday’s value area, the auction closed with a downward spike in the M-period, creating a disconnect between price and value. Tuesday’s poor low was cleaned up in the process.

Contextually, not much has changed. Continued value development within Tuesday’s range and acceptance of the lower prices created by today’s spike would be a bearish development. A very weak market will accept below the key 7718 level, aligning with the Monthly VWAP.

Negating the closing weakness by reclaiming today’s spike base at 7747, which also aligns with the current weekly VWAP, would signal short-term strength.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is at **7718**, the prior gap, which aligns with the Monthly VWAP. Holding below 7718 would maintain downside pressure and target the HVN at **7687 (DT1)**, where a potential LBAF is of interest. Acceptance below 7687 would signal intraday weakness, targeting **7669 (DT2)**, with **7640 (FDT)**, the Weekly Extreme Low, serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.

**On the flip side**, reclaiming 7718 shifts focus to today’s spike base at **7747 (UT1)**, where a potential LAAF is of interest, with the HVN at **7772 (FUT)** serving as the final upside target under sustained buying pressure.
