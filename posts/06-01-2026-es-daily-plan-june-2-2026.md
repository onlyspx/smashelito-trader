Notable absorption early in the session led to a squeeze in the direction of the prevailing trend. The key 7633 level was reached in the process, from which the auction pulled back, forming an excess high that was well auctioned from an order flow perspective.

As excess often marks the end of one auction and the start of another, the key in the short term will be to monitor sellers’ ability to gain acceptance back within last week‘s range.

Intraday strength would be indicated by building value above 7629 (UT1), negating today’s excess, while weakness would be signaled by a break and hold below 7585 (DT1), i.e. traction back within Thursday’s range.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is **7611**, last week’s high. Holding above 7611 signals stability and targets **7629 (UT1)**. Acceptance above 7629 would signal intraday strength, targeting **7661 (UT2)**, with a final upside target at **7680 (FUT)** under sustained buying pressure.

**On the flip side**, failure to hold **7611** would shift focus to **7585 (DT1)**. Acceptance below 7585 would signal intraday weakness, targeting **7562 (DT2)**, with a final downside target at **7537 (FDT)** under sustained selling pressure.
