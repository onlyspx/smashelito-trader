### Contextual Analysis & Plan

The market made a new all-time high overnight at 7223.25, which was not retested during regular trading hours and is therefore carried forward as unfinished business. A Look Above and Fail at 7216 (UT1) led to a return within Monday’s range and a test of the Smashlevel at 7200. This level represented both Monday’s LVN and last week’s high, a key area sellers needed to break in order to introduce short-term weakness. Sellers achieved this overnight and then defended 7200 during the European session, triggering a downside continuation (see **Figure 1**).

Friday’s directional move from balance and Monday’s continuation were rejected today, triggering a return to value. The return to the high volume node (HVN) at 7160s implies the auction is in a balanced state heading into tomorrow’s FOMC and tech earnings. Carry forward the untested overnight ATH at 7223.25 as unfinished business.

Intraday strength would be indicated by a reclaim of 7180 (UT1), the low volume node (LVN), while weakness would be signaled by a break and hold below 7126 (DT1), the prior multi-day value support.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is **7156**, the HVN. Holding above 7156 signals stability and targets the LVN at **7180 (UT1)**. Acceptance above 7180 would signal intraday strength, targeting **7208 (UT2)**, with a final upside target at **7230 (FUT)** under sustained buying pressure.

**On the flip side**, failure to hold **7156** would shift focus to **7126 (DT1)**, with a final downside target at **7092 (FDT)**, the HTF level, under sustained selling pressure.
