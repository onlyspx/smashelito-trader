### Contextual Analysis & Plan

Sellers made early attempts at downside continuation overnight by breaking below Monday’s lower distribution. Both attempts ultimately failed to break the multi-day balance low, instead triggering responsive buyers and a rotation higher. Sellers initially defended 7497 (Smashlevel), but once buyers reclaimed the level and negated the most bearish scenario, we got a great move toward 7530 (UT1), where sellers stepped in. 7530 proved to be a solid level, producing good reactions overnight (ON high: 7530.25) and early in RTH.

The upside was capped by 7530 almost to the tick during the A-period (7530.25), with VIX simultaneously testing its support level at 17.58, providing great confluence. If we have to nitpick, the ideal scenario would have been a stronger probe above 7530 before reversing, rather than simply tagging the overnight high to the tick. As outlined in the plan, reclaiming 7530 was bullish intraday, and once buyers gained traction above the level, it opened the door to a revisit of 7548 (FUT). It was interesting to observe how the VIX lost its support level at 17.58 before buyers triggered continuation above 7530. The 7548 level capped today’s upside, which should come as no surprise since it’s a crucial short-term inflection point.

Keep in mind that reclaiming a level and shifting the “intraday state” to bullish does not mean buyers have regained control of the auction overall. It simply means the door is open to bullish continuation intraday. Contextually, buyers still need to reclaim 7548 to negate Friday’s shift in tone (see **Figure** **1**), so these concepts should not be conflated.

Monday’s double distribution trend day was rejected today, triggering a rotation back toward last week’s gap area.

While buyers were in firm control of today’s auction, acceptance back above 7548, where notable buying effort was observed today, is still required to negate Friday’s shift in tone, which has yet to be achieved.

Intraday strength would be indicated by a reclaim of 7578 (UT1), Thursday’s VAL, while weakness would be signaled by a break and hold below 7530 (DT1), today’s halfback, trapping today’s buying effort in the process.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is at **7548**, Thursday’s low. Holding below 7548 maintains downside pressure, targeting **7530 (DT1)**. Acceptance below 7530 would signal intraday weakness, targeting **7510 (DT2)**, with **7485 (FDT)** serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.

**On the flip side**, reclaiming and holding above **7548** shifts focus to Thursday’s VAL at **7578 (UT1)**, with **7605 (FUT)** serving as the final upside target under sustained buying pressure.
