### Contextual Analysis & Plan

Sellers attempted to break and hold below 7706 (DT1) during the Asian session but ultimately failed to gain downside traction. During the European session, the auction consolidated within Tuesday’s range before headlines triggered a notable spike higher, tagging 7750 (UT1) in the process. Heading into the RTH session, we had a Look Below and Fail (LBAF) of 7706 and a Look Above and Fail (LAAF) of 7750.

The RTH session opened with a true gap up following the overnight strength. The overnight spike was fully retraced during the A-period, with the auction filling the gap and testing 7726 (Smashlevel), where responsive buyers stepped in. The failure to gain traction within Tuesday’s value area resulted in bullish continuation, with the Initial Balance extending to the upside. Notably, buyers came a few ticks shy of filling Tuesday’s true gap down at 7766.25 (HOD: 7764.75). The failure to fully close the gap suggests that stronger buyers were absent today. During the afternoon session, the auction returned to the Initial Balance range and consolidated, with the session ultimately closing back within Tuesday’s range. The market-generated information is a bit conflicting now, making trading conditions tricky. The daily is back to balance after breaking the pattern of lower highs and lower lows.
