### Contextual Analysis & Plan

The immediate area of interest today was Wednesday’s spike area, highlighted on the chart. A weak market would accept the lower prices established by the spike by defending its base at 7371 (Smashlevel). The next level for sellers to defend was 7398 (UT1), as failure to do so would put Wednesday’s breakdown into question. Overnight, the main focus was on 7371, which buyers took control of during the Euro session, with VIX actually breaking below its support level at 19.56. The 7398 level initially slowed upside momentum, but buyers reclaimed it ahead of the RTH session, signaling weak sellers in the context of Wednesday’s breakdown.

During the RTH session, buyers defended the 7398 level twice: first immediately in the A-period, and then again during the D- and E-periods, where the auction formed a poor low. There was little reason to maintain a bearish intraday bias, as sellers’ failure to defend 7371 and 7398 opened the door for a rotation back toward value.

This is how the auction process works: when the market moves away from balance (value), the key question is whether those new prices are accepted or rejected. Acceptance indicates that the market is willing to do business at the new price levels, allowing the move to continue. Rejection signals a lack of participation and often results in a rotation back toward prior value.
