We can essentially treat the low-volume area (LVA) below last week’s VAL as a spike. Trading within or below this LVA (acceptance) would be a bearish development, while rejecting Friday’s weakness by trading back above the LVA would open the door to a return to value.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is at **7410**, Friday’s halfback, aligning with last week’s VAL. Holding below 7410 maintains downside pressure, targeting Friday’s excess base at **7369 (DT1)**, with **7335 (FDT)** serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.

**On the flip side**, reclaiming and holding above **7410** would shift focus to last week’s VPOC at **7444 (UT1)**, with **7472 (FUT)** serving as the final upside target under sustained buying pressure.
