### Contextual Analysis & Plan

In contrast to Monday and Tuesday, we saw some volatility today. Overnight, buyers initially defended 7848 (DT1) before reclaiming 7866 (Smashlevel) ahead of the release. However, the tone shifted significantly after CPI, with sellers taking control and setting the stage for the RTH session to open with a true gap down.

A **True Gap** in Market Profile occurs when the new session opens completely above or below the prior day’s entire trading range, meaning there is no overlap in traded prices between the two sessions.

A **Normal (settlement) gap** is when price opens away from the prior day’s close, but can still overlap or trade within the prior day’s range, meaning there is no structural separation in the auction.

A true gap is generally considered more significant, as it reflects a clearer shift in market tone and a potential transition into a new auction area.

When the auction opens with a true gap in either direction, it signals a shift in tone relative to the prior session. Our job is then to assess whether that shift will be accepted or rejected. Buyers initially defended the opening level, allowing the auction to fill today’s gap at 7834.50, where sellers stepped in. This classic gap-fill reversal provided an early clue of weakness, as the potential shift in tone remained intact. Subsequently, sellers broke below the overnight low, providing a second clue of weakness.

The final downside target at 7807 (FDT) was already tagged in the B-period. At the time, the VIX was still holding below its resistance at 15.94. However, value was building below 7807, negating the potential for a reversal. A quick LBAF at 7807 would have been a different story. The Initial Balance was then extended to the downside in the C-period, with the VIX breaking above its resistance later in the session, which typically signals the potential for continued weakness.

The true gap at 7741.25 was filled almost to the tick in the J-period, marking the session low in confluence with the MA50 (**see Figure 1**). If you have reviewed the [Weekly Plan](https://newsletter.smashelito.com/p/es-weekly-plan-october-12-16-2026), you know that weekly support lies between 7740 and 7710, meaning we are now at a critical area for buyers to defend. Failure to hold this area would open the door to a rotation down to the multi-week balance low, following the failed breakout.
