The context remains unchanged following today’s rather uneventful inside day. The auction remained contained between our two key levels: 7866, last week’s VAH, and 7848, the prior ATH and multi-week balance high. Both remain key references to monitor in the short term.

Reclaiming 7866 would be a short-term bullish development, opening the door to a traverse of Tuesday’s value area. Failure to defend 7848 would open the door to a rotation lower toward last week’s VAL at 7807, which closely aligns with the Monthly VWAP.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is at **7866**, last week’s VAH, which aligns with the prior SPX ATH (7816.17). Holding below 7866 would target the prior ATH at **7848 (DT1)**, where a potential LBAF is of interest. Acceptance below 7848 would signal intraday weakness, targeting **7830 (DT1)**, with **7807 (FDT)**, last week’s VAL, serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.

**On the flip side**, reclaiming and holding above **7866** would shift focus to Tuesday’s IB high at **7887 (UT1)**, where a potential LAAF is of interest, with **7915 (FUT)** serving as the final upside target under sustained buying pressure.
