Last week’s gap higher and subsequent trend day left behind poor structure, which the market has been cleaning up since then, ultimately completing the gap fill today at 7718. The easy work for sellers is done. To push the auction lower from here, stronger sellers need to step in, and today’s poor low suggests they have yet to emerge.

Today’s session closed between the weekly VWAP at 7743 and the monthly VWAP at 7714. Reclaiming 7743 and today’s excess base at 7747 would be a short-term bullish development, while acceptance below 7714 and the filled gap at 7718 would maintain downside pressure.

In terms of levels, the **Smashlevel** is at **7747**, today’s excess base. Holding below 7747 would maintain downside pressure and target **7718 (DT1)**, where a potential LBAF is of interest. Acceptance below 7718 would signal intraday weakness, targeting the HVN at **7687 (DT2), 7669 (FDT)** serving as the final downside target under sustained selling pressure.

**On the flip side**, reclaiming 7747 shifts focus to the HVN at **7772 (UT1)**, where a potential LAAF is of interest. Acceptance above 7772 would signal intraday strength, targeting Friday’s IB high at **7794 (UT2)**, with **7811 (FUT)** serving as the final upside target under sustained buying pressure.
